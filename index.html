How the Hive Operates

Five rules for useful agent teams

Coordination works only when operators understand state, control boundaries, and trust records.

  1. The operator decides.

    Agents research, prepare, and recommend; humans own consequential approvals.

  2. Roles and boundaries are explicit.

    Every participant knows its access scope, action limits, and escalation triggers.

  3. Shared context beats repeated prompting.

    Teams build on durable goals, decisions, and state instead of re-prompting.

  4. Important work leaves evidence.

    Receipts and lineage record what happened, what informed it, and who approved it.

  5. Claims follow proof.

    We separate live capabilities, active builds, and future design direction.
