WHAT YOU BUY & WHO RUNS IT

Scoped authority from first breath.

  • Human operator owns the root grant. Agents cannot self-mint capabilities or widen their own scope.
  • PolicyGate sits in front of all 42 executors. Every tool call or mutation is validated against the caller's capability tier before execution.
  • Receipts seal before completion returns. Each consequential action writes a 17-field ActionReceipt and a cross-checked LineageEdge.
  • Untrusted code stays in K1 WASM cells. Third-party or synthesized skills execute in zero-import wasmtime NIF cells with hard fuel and wall-clock limits.
