WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

Three pillars grounded in running code.

  • 1. Governed execution substrate (Thunderline & ThunderBlock) — Every consequential agent action passes through PolicyGate.validate_and_execute/2 across 42 Crown executor modules and 4 explicit capability tiers (:read, :mutate, :admin, :execute).
  • 2. Byte-verifiable receipt & lineage spine — Every authorized action emits a sealed ActionReceipt over 17 canonical @seal_fields paired with a persisted LineageEdge (verify_persisted_edge/4), turning auditor provenance questions into deterministic SQL queries.
  • 3. Hermetic runtime & live reference stack — Untrusted skill execution runs inside zero-import K1WasmCell sandboxes (wasmtime NIF with fuel and wall-clock bounds), observed through Prism (live) and proven in workflows by ACORN (live).

WHAT WE'RE NOT

Explicit non-claims.

  • No SOC2 Type II audit yet; roadmap targets Q1 2027. We do not claim third-party compliance certification ahead of audit completion.
  • Seals are unkeyed; they raise the price of tampering, not forbid it. Per AUTHORITY_LINEAGE_KEYED_SEAL_V0, receipts and lineage edges cross-verify each other; keyed HMAC/signature sealing is tracked openly (GH #3173).
  • Thunderline is not AGI; it governs agent actions, not agent reasoning. No regulatory-compliance guarantee. Thunderline is designed for governed workflows; your compliance posture remains yours.

PRODUCT CANON HIERARCHY

How the stack fits together.

Thunderline is the governed operating environment. A ThunderBlock is one managed workspace inside it. Prism is the operator surface. ACORN is the first live application proving the stack.
