This posture is what regulated buyers — federal contractors, defense integrators, finance, healthcare, legal discovery — need but cannot buy today from the OpenAI Enterprise, Anthropic for Work, or AWS Bedrock stack. Our first live commercial application is ACORN, government-contracting intelligence at a private demo hosted for early operator use.

Thunderline governs who and what may act. Omega5 makes the machine, program, state, and evidence behind that action explicit and reproducible.
